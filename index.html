AI safety monitoring for construction teams

Anzen detects PPE violations before risk turns into injury.

A YOLO-powered PPE kit detection system that monitors helmet, vest, glove, goggle, and mask compliance from site cameras, then turns every violation into clear dashboard action.

View demo metrics

Why Anzen

Manual PPE checks do not scale with busy sites.

Detect missing PPE

Identify missing helmets, vests, goggles, masks, and gloves from camera frames using model predictions.

Track who needs attention

Use temporary worker IDs until permanent identification is added, keeping the workflow useful during development.

Act quickly

Show severity, location, camera, confidence, and history so supervisors can respond without digging through footage.

How it works

From camera frame to safety record.

01

Capture

Site cameras or uploaded clips send frames to the backend detection service.

02

Detect

YOLO runs PPE class detection from Roboflow-trained datasets and returns labels, boxes, and confidence.

03

Identify

Temporary worker IDs group repeated detections until face, badge, or tracker-based identity is ready.

04

Respond

The dashboard creates violation records, severity levels, alerts, and searchable history.
